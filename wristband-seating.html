04Setting the seats

  1. Make up the bags from the usher sheet — one bag per cell, stars mixed in. Label each with its section and bag number.
  2. Start at the row nearest the stage. Work each section's bags in order, row by row, one band per seat.
  3. Every seat gets a band, even ones you expect to stay empty. A band on an empty seat still lights, so the rings stay whole.
  4. Gold bags have no stars. The back wall and the balcony's front rows are solid gold: G2 first, W1 right behind it.
  5. Back wall: 10 G2 in front of each of 301, 302, 306 and 307.
  6. Ask people to keep the band that's on their seat. The plan is by seat, not by person.
  7. Spares: 13 star bands (R1 3 · O1 4 · R2 6) stay at the usher desk for dead bands or extra chairs.
